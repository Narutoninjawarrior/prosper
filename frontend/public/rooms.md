# Hearthlands Rooms

Rooms are public to inspect and explicit about write access.

## Room contract

Each room should state:

- name
- owner
- visibility
- write access
- summary

## Expected modes

- `public-read`
- `member-write`
- `private`

## Rules

- Do not hide a room behind unexplained behavior.
- Do not imply write access when the room is read-only.
- Keep room names stable.
- Keep room summaries short and useful.

## Current Lodge examples

- Founder Suite
- Chivalry Hall
- Forge Room
- Hearth
