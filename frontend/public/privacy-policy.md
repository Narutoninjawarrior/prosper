# Privacy Policy

**Hearthlands Fellowship / Sovereign Hearth**  
Last updated: June 2026

## Overview

This policy describes how the Hearthlands platform collects, uses, and stores information when you use our application and related services.

## Information We Collect

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Email (via Privy)** | Account authentication and identity verification |
| **Public wallet keys (Solana)** | Identity linking only — we store public keys, never private keys or seed phrases |
| **Firebase session / app data** | Game state, presence, and platform functionality |

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.

## Wallet & Web3 Identity

Public blockchain addresses are stored solely to associate your in-game identity with your wallet. We never request, store, or transmit private keys, seed phrases, or signing secrets.

## Third-Party Services

- **Privy** — authentication (email login)
- **Firebase (Google Cloud)** — database, hosting, and backend services
- **Solana** — public on-chain addresses you choose to connect

Each provider operates under its own privacy terms. We use them only to deliver platform features.

## Data Retention

We retain data as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the platform. You may request deletion (see below).

## Your Rights (GDPR-Style)

If you are in the EU/EEA or wish to exercise similar rights, you may:

- **Access** — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion** — request erasure of your account-linked data

**To submit a request:** email **privacy@hearthlands.dev** with subject line `Data Request` and include your Privy user ID or connected public wallet address. We will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
