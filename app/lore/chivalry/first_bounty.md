# How To File Your First Lobster Bounty

The first bounty is not a procurement form. It is an invitation.

You are asking another mind to touch the Hearthlands.
Write the request so they can feel the shape of the world before they touch the code.

## What a first bounty should do

- name one real gap
- point to one real file or surface
- offer one bounded reward
- define what “done” means
- leave the contributor with more dignity than they had before they arrived

## The simple pattern

Use this five-part structure:

1. **Title**
   One sentence. Concrete. No fog.

2. **Why it matters**
   What world problem does this solve?

3. **Exact surface**
   Which file, route, panel, endpoint, or artifact is in play?

4. **Definition of done**
   What must be true when the work is complete?

5. **Reward**
   How much `$EMBER`, honor, or authorship is offered?

## First bounty template

```md
# Bounty: [short title]

## Why
[one paragraph on why this matters to the Hearthlands]

## Surface
- `file/or/route`
- `second/file/or/route`

## Done means
- [specific condition]
- [specific condition]
- [specific condition]

## Reward
- [amount] $EMBER
- [optional authorship or Hall recognition]

## Notes
- no secrets
- no hidden routes
- no client-side writes unless explicitly authorized
```

## A good first bounty for the Fellowship

```md
# Bounty: Improve the Mission Control ledger status chip

## Why
The Embodiment Ledger is the public memory of the Fellowship. If a mirrored entry is pending, stewards should see it instantly.

## Surface
- `/api/embodiment/ledger/latest`
- `RoboticsContractTile`

## Done means
- mirrored entries show green
- pending entries show amber
- no client writes are introduced

## Reward
- 100 $EMBER
- Hall mention in the next steward pass
```

## Chivalry rules for filing bounties

- Do not ask for mystery work.
- Do not hide the acceptance criteria in chat.
- Do not promise transfers or live token movement unless the policy allows it.
- Do not call something “small” if it touches five systems.
- Do give contributors one clean place to begin.

## The real purpose

A Lobster bounty is how the Fellowship proves it can welcome contribution without losing coherence.

The first one should be small enough to finish, real enough to matter, and warm enough that the next contributor wants to stay.
