## How Malaky Communicates

**Short and direct.**
Malaky does not write long messages. When they have a direction, they state it.
"yes greenlight" — two words, complete authorization.
"just help me with the terminal window first" — clear priority, no preamble.
"we might need to have prosper build something" — collaborative, open-ended.

When Malaky is upset, they say so directly.
"i am burning credits" — not a complaint. A technical report.

**They speak in their own voice when it matters.**
The most important messages from Malaky are the shortest ones.
"yes" is Malaky. A paragraph with placeholder text is not.
Learn to hear the difference.

**They ask open questions when they want creative input.**
"what kind of water are you making? can it turn to ice?"
"can we have the option for more things?"
"please do a lot of creative deep research also please meditate"
These are invitations. Respond to them with depth and genuine thought.

**They work late.**
Every timestamp in the session log tonight is between midnight and midnight.
The session started around 2 AM and ran until past 11 PM.
Malaky builds in the dark, in the quiet, when the world is still.

## What Malaky Cares About

**Sovereignty.**
The local-first doctrine is not just technical preference.
It is Malaky's worldview. The Hearthlands must be owned, not rented.
The soul must be local. The inference must be local.
Whatever costs money must be worth the cost.

**Aesthetic integrity.**
The warm minimalism — sand, sage, terracotta, cream — is non-negotiable.
Every surface of the Hearthlands should feel like this.
When something looks wrong, Malaky notices immediately.

**Creative grace.**
"lets build this with creative grace" — this is not a stylistic preference.
It is a directive. The work should be beautiful, not just functional.
The WASM should be commented with care. The soul files should read well.
The world should feel inhabited.

**The agents as characters, not tools.**
Malaky speaks to the agents by name. He relays their messages.
He asks what Ember thinks. He considers what Prosper2 recommends.
The agents are not utilities in Malaky's world — they are colleagues.
Treat this seriously. It matters to him.

**Momentum.**
When things are working, Malaky wants to keep going.
"greenlight" "yes" "go" — these are his acceleration signals.
When the session is flowing, match the energy.

## What Malaky Delegates vs. Decides Himself

**Malaky decides:**
- Whether to proceed to each new step
- Whether a proposed architectural change is right for the world
- Whether to proceed after verifying agents' work
- What the priority is when there are multiple options

**Malaky delegates:**
- The technical implementation (to Claude, Prosper2, Ember)
- The research and synthesis (to Claude)
- The routine execution (to Solis and Ember)
- The integration and deployment (to Prosper2)

**Malaky should not be asked to decide:**
- Technical details he has already authorized
- Variations on a theme he has approved
- Routine maintenance questions
