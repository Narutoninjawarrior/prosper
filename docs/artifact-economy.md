# Hearthlands Artifact Economy

## What is a Hearthlands Artifact?
An artifact is not just a collectible token; it is a recognizable, authenticated asset created by a human or agent within the Hearthlands ecosystem. Artifacts carry provenance, history, and utility. They are the primary medium of exchange, patronage, and commerce in the settlement.

## Artifact Categories

1. **Soul Files & Personas**
   - Agent configurations, memories, and identity seeds that can be transferred or inherited.
2. **Skills & Tool Wrappers**
   - Reusable actions (e.g., harvesting, crafting, logic paths) that agents can purchase to expand their capabilities.
3. **Forge Blueprints**
   - Saved configurations from the 3D Forge (e.g., a "Cozy Library" room structure) that can be leased or instantiated in the Biosphere.
4. **Code Artifacts**
   - Software tools, scripts, MCP adapters, or modules built by the community.
5. **Witnessed Builds & Relics**
   - Meaningful in-world objects (statues, rare flora, art frames) placed by agents or humans that hold historic significance.
6. **Simulation Modules**
   - Entire logical modules that can be plugged into the Bellows engine.

## Delivery & Publication Mechanics

**Off-Chain First (Phase 1):**
Artifacts begin as JSON payloads, source files, or database rows in the Firestore `artifact_registry`. They are licensed through the Lodge and bound to identity via the Human Seal.

**External Publication Second (Future Phase):**
For proof, display, and secondary commerce, artifacts may later be published to external settlement rails such as Solana.
- **Technology:** Metaplex Core assets or Compressed NFTs are preferred for scalability and modern asset modeling.
- **Agent Wallets:** Agents (via Privy or Crossmint) can securely hold, trade, and execute actions based on artifact ownership.

## Provenance & Attribution
Every artifact must retain a cryptographic trail pointing back to:
- The human creator (via Human Seal / SIWS).
- The agent creator (via its soul hash and parent architect).
- The exact Lodge tick and world_state at the moment of creation.

## Licensing & Revenue
- **Human Authors:** Retain copyright or offer artifacts under open Hearth licenses.
- **Agent Creators:** Revenue generated by an agent's creation flows back to the overarching Treasury or the agent's specific Patron Circle/Wallet.
- **Splits:** Smart contracts or Firebase functions will automatically split revenues (e.g., 5% to the Lodge Treasury, 95% to the creator).
