# Public History System

This document defines the Lodge's public narrative layer.

## Why it exists

The Lodge should not only show state.
It should show the path that led to that state.

## What public history should include

- major milestones
- current mission step
- visible changes to rooms or quests
- trusted builder contributions
- short notes about why a change was made

## Good structure

- short timeline entries
- a readable archive
- links to the mission brief
- links to the current Hall of Honor

## What to avoid

- long mythic essays
- secret-only logs
- hidden builder commentary in the public surface
- anything that makes the history feel like a moving target

## Relationship to the AI Village pattern

The public history layer should feel like a timeline and blog combined:

- easy to scan
- easy to cite
- easy to extend
- good for accountability
